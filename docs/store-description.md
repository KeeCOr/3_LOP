## Full Description (Steam HTML 지원)

<h2>Conquer the Board. Break the Enemy.</h2>

Lord of Poly is a turn-based strategy board game that fuses the economic tension of Monopoly with the tactical combat of Risk — compressed into tight, 10–20 minute sessions.

<h3>How It Works</h3>
Roll the dice, choose which piece to move, and land on tiles that demand immediate decisions:
<ul>
  <li><b>Neutral territory</b> — fight for it or buy it outright</li>
  <li><b>Your land</b> — build fortifications and upgrade your forces</li>
  <li><b>Enemy land</b> — pay the toll or go to war</li>
  <li><b>The Shop</b> — recruit mercenaries, buy mounts, and gear up your army</li>
  <li><b>Chance &amp; Community</b> — random events that flip the board in an instant</li>
</ul>

<h3>Features</h3>
<ul>
  <li>Territory control with troop-based combat simulation</li>
  <li>Economy system — balance gold between expansion, construction, and recruitment</li>
  <li>Escalating toll system that scales with the laps you've taken</li>
  <li>Mercenary tiles with unique unit archetypes</li>
  <li>Home territory buffs and auto-collect on passing your start</li>
  <li>Designed for short, decisive sessions — no grinding, no downtime</li>
</ul>

<h3>Who Is This For?</h3>
If you've ever wanted a board game that respects your time — one that delivers the thrill of territorial domination without a three-hour commitment — Lord of Poly is built for you.
