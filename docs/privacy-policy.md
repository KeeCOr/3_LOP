# Privacy Policy — Lord of Poly

**Last updated: 2026-08-30**

## Overview

Lord of Poly is a single-player desktop game. This policy describes what data is (and is not) collected.

## Data We Do NOT Collect

Lord of Poly does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. Specifically:

- No account registration or login required
- No names, email addresses, or contact information collected
- No gameplay telemetry or analytics sent to any server
- No crash reports uploaded automatically
- No advertising identifiers used

## Local Save Data

Game progress is saved **locally on your device only** using standard file system storage. This data never leaves your computer and is not accessible to us.

## Third-Party Services

Lord of Poly does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or social platforms.

**Steam**: If you purchased Lord of Poly via Steam, Valve Corporation's [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/) applies to your Steam account data. We receive no personal data from Steam.

## Children

Lord of Poly does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.
